# bank-card

## HK bank cards

| Bank               | Note                                                                        |
| ------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Ele Bank           | Better forex rate, and remittance to mainland with fixed commission 30 HKD. |
| Ant Bank           | Direct transfer to Alipay, but with bad forex rate.                         |
| ZA Bank            | Lower transaction freeze risk, so can be used as a receiving account.       |
| Bank of China (HK) | Remittance to mainland without fee. (Remittance -> BOC Remittance Plus)     |
| Hang Seng Bank     | Global free transfers.                                                      |

## Forex

Mainland, RMB -> USD: Buy from Xing Ye Huan Yu Bank, then transfer to HSBC HK Bank or Hang Seng Bank (Best) > Bank of China HK (Worst).

HK, HKD -> USD (Bank): Ele Bank (Best) > Hang Seng Bank > Bank of China HK > ZA Bank > HSBC HK Bank > Chase Bank (Worst).

HK, HKD -> USD (Broker): IBKR (Best) > Fusion > Futu > Tiger > Long Bridge > uSmart (Worst).

HK, HKD -> USD (All): IBKR (Best) > Fusion > Futu > Tiger > Ele Bank > Long Bridge > uSmart > Hang Seng Bank > Bank of China HK > ZA Bank > HSBC HK Bank > Chase Bank (Worst).
